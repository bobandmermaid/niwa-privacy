# Privacy Policy for Niwa (iOS)

**Last updated:** October 2026

Niwa ("we", "our", or "the App") is an autonomous, offline plant care companion designed with a privacy-first approach. We believe your plant care routines and personal data belong entirely to you.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
* **No Personal Data Collected:** Niwa does not collect, track, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) on external servers.
* **No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers:** The App contains no advertising frameworks, tracking scripts, or analytics SDKs (no Google Firebase, no Meta SDK, etc.).
* **No User Accounts:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address to use the App.

### 2. On-Device Local Storage
* All information you create in the App — including your plants, watering schedules, care logs, photos, and notes — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's native **SwiftData** framework.
* If you create a backup, the JSON file is generated locally and is only transferred if you explicitly share it via AirDrop, iCloud Drive, or the iOS Files app.

### 3. Device Permissions
Niwa requests access only to features strictly necessary for its functionality:
* **Notifications (`UNUserNotificationCenter`):** Used solely to schedule local, predictive on-device reminders when a plant needs watering. No notification payload is ever sent over the internet.
* **Photo Library (`PhotosUI`):** Used only when you choose to attach photos of your plants to their growth timeline. Photos remain stored securely on your device.

### 4. Data Retention and Deletion
Since all data resides on your physical iPhone:
* You can delete individual plants, photos, or logs at any time directly in the App.
* Deleting the App from your device permanently removes all stored data.

### 5. Disclaimer of Care Advice
Niwa is designed as an informational and educational tool to assist with home plant care routines. Care intervals, drought thresholds, and fertilizer dosages are calculated estimates based on botanical models and user-provided parameters. Environmental conditions vary between individual homes. The user assumes full responsibility for evaluating soil moisture and making care decisions.

### 6. Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or need support, contact the developer:
* **Email:** bobandmermaid@rocketmail.com

## Политика конфиденциальности (Русская версия)

Приложение **Niwa** создано по принципу полной локальной автономности:
1. **Сбор данных:** Приложение не собирает, не анализирует и не передает ваши данные сторонним серверам или аналитическим сервисам. В приложении нет рекламы и трекеров.
2. **Хранение:** Все данные коллекции, фотографии, график полива и история ухода хранятся исключительно в памяти вашего устройства через Apple SwiftData.
3. **Разрешения:** Уведомления и доступ к фото используются локально и только для напоминаний о поливе и фотохроники.
4. **Удаление данных:** Удаление записей в приложении или удаление самого приложения стирает все сохраненные данные.
